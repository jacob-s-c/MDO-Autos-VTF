Independent test-fit inspection for replacement vehicle parts.

MDO Autos installs a candidate part alongside a genuine OEM part on the same undamaged vehicle, measures the difference, and documents it. Manufacturers and suppliers use our reports to certify fit, resolve field complaints, and monitor production quality over time.

Four inspection types

Each type answers a different question about a part. They share the same measurement method and differ in what triggers them and what the result is used for.

01

Certification VTF

Required before a candidate part earns independent fit certification. The part is measured directly against its OEM equivalent on the same vehicle position.

 Triggered before certification 02

Complaint VTF

Opened when a shop reports a fit problem with a part that already holds certification. Establishes whether the cause sits in the part, the vehicle, or the installation.

 Triggered by a field complaint 03

Marketplace Monitoring VTF

A spot-check on parts purchased from the open market rather than supplied by the manufacturer, confirming that shipping production still matches what was certified.

 Ongoing surveillance 04

Dimensional Inspection VTF

A voluntary inspection a manufacturer can request at any time on a certified or candidate part, without opening a certification decision or a complaint file.

 On request, any time
02

How an inspection works

Every inspection type runs the same four stages. The controlled variable is the part; everything else is held constant so that any deviation is attributable.

01

Source the vehicle

We obtain an undamaged donor vehicle for the platform and record baseline measurements with the factory part still fitted, before anything is removed.

02

Install both parts

The candidate part and a genuine OEM part are fitted to the same position in sequence, by the same technician, using factory torque and sequence.

03

Measure and compare

Gap, flush, hole alignment, bracket engagement, surface condition, and install time are recorded for both parts under identical conditions.

04

Report and resolve

You receive a dimensional report with photographic evidence and, where fit deviates from the OEM reference, the measurements needed to correct it.
